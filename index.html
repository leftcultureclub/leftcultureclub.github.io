Autonomous, for everyone.

Welcome to the Left Culture Club! If you’re tired of social events that never go below the surface, craving real community, and curious about exploring radical politics in a safe, friendly and non-judgemental space, you’ve just found it!

At LCC, you’ll find film screenings, skill-shares, games nights, book clubs and open conversations that don't require a party line, only empathy and curiosity.

We’re building the social fabric the left has been missing - and you are invited to be part of it.
